- Added `Update.sections()` and `Release.sections()`, which split release notes at their Markdown headings into `Section`s with a `Section.Kind`: the headings of Keep a Changelog (Added, Changed, Deprecated, Removed, Fixed, Security) and those written for Conventional Commits (Features, Bug Fixes, BREAKING CHANGES, …). `Update.sections()` merges them over every release of the update, breaking changes first. Unknown headings keep their own section of kind `OTHER`, and text under no heading forms the `OTHER` section with an empty heading.
